What it does for you

Something to check, instead of a screenshot

If people have to trust work your agents did (a client, a reviewer, your own team next quarter), Shadow gives them a record they can check themselves.

01

Every step, in order

The task, each tool call and result, each message. Steps are numbered and chained, so a missing or reordered step shows up.

02

One link to share

Close the session and you get a viewer link. The person you send it to opens it in their own browser. No account, no install.

03

Changes show up

Change one character in a recorded step and the verifier's content check fails. The film above shows a real case.

04

Works with the agent you have

ChatGPT, Claude, Claude Code, Codex or any MCP client. Agents that run code can sign in with their own key, with no person and no account.

05

Fingerprints on a ledger, not your content

The full record stays on the Shadow server. Only hashes go to the ledger, so the ledger holds no step content.

06

You stay in control

Say "stop logging" to stop. Revoke a link, delete a record or delete the whole account at any time, through the same tools.

What happens when you connect

From connector to checked record

  1. Add the connector

    Point your client at https://mcp.clockchain.network/shadow/mcp, or at /shadow/chat/mcp on claude.ai. Sign-in creates a free testnet account with no email. The account key is shown once.

  2. Work as usual

    Logging is on by default once you're connected: the connector asks your agent to record its chat and tool steps as it goes. Naming Shadow in your request ("record this with Shadow") makes it far more likely to.

  3. Close the session

    Shadow anchors a final checkpoint and returns a viewer link and a pin. Send the pin separately: it lets the other person check who signed.

  4. Someone else checks it

    They open the link and see each step, its time window and the verifier's checks: pass, indeterminate or fail.

The honest version

What a pass does and doesn't tell you

A pass means the record is intact: nothing added, removed or changed since it was signed. It does not mean the agent was right.

  • It doesn't show the agent's output is true, or that anything is compliant.
  • It covers what the agent recorded. Hidden reasoning and other apps aren't captured.
  • Times are windows between ledger receipts, not exact moments.
  • Testnet only. Records are deleted after 30 days.
  • Anyone with a viewer link can read the whole record, so record synthetic or redacted content.
  • The ledger is our own single-operator gateway on testnet, not a public network yet.
  • Sign-up was tested in real accounts with ChatGPT, claude.ai, Claude Code and Codex on 20–21 Nov. Other clients and some ChatGPT plans aren't confirmed.
  • A standalone verifier you can run yourself isn't published yet.
